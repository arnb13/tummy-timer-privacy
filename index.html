Privacy Policy — Tummy Timer

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Tummy Timer does not collect any data about you or your baby. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Tummy Timer is a baby feeding tracker for Android, published by Nafis Hasrat Arnob.

What the app stores

Everything you log is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your device:

  • Feeds — the time, whether it was breast or bottle, and any amount, duration or note you add
  • Pumping sessions and the freezer total
  • Growth measurements — weight, height and the date
  • Your baby's name and date of birth, if you choose to enter them
  • Your settings — the gap between feeds, the reminder mode and the theme

Every one of these is optional except the feed times themselves, and none of it leaves your device.

What the app sends

Nothing. Tummy Timer has no internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere, and it does not contain any analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting service.

You can confirm this: put the phone in aeroplane mode and the app works exactly as it does otherwise.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. We have no way to identify you, and no copy of your data exists anywhere but your own phone.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

PermissionWhy it is needed
NotificationsTo show the reminder when the next feed is due
Run at startupTo restore a pending reminder after the phone restarts
VibrateSo the reminder can vibrate if you choose that setting

Tummy Timer deliberately asks for no storage permission. When you export a backup or a spreadsheet, Android's own file picker asks you where to save it and grants the app access to that one file only. When you restore a backup, the same thing happens in reverse.

Backups you create

If you use Export backup or Export spreadsheet, the file is written wherever you choose — a folder on your phone, a cloud drive, an email. Once it leaves the app it is yours, and it is governed by whatever service you saved it to, not by us. We never see it.

Android's own backup

Android may include the app's data in the device backup tied to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That is a function of your phone and your Google account settings, not of Tummy Timer, and it is covered by Google's privacy policy. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Children's privacy

Tummy Timer is intended for use by parents and carers, who are adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. The app records information about an infant only because a parent chooses to type it in, and that information never leaves their phone.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device, so you are always in control:

  • Delete individual feeds by swiping them away in History
  • Replace the whole history with Restore backup in Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes the database and every setting with it

There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never had it.

Changes to this policy
